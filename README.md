# Indian Market Breadth

**[Open the page →](https://markets.ashwinraghavan.com/)**

A free daily view of the Indian stock market's health: how many of the 1,000 most-traded NSE stocks are rising or falling, at 52-week highs or lows, and above their 50-day average, with sector strength, India VIX against the Nifty's actual swings, MCX commodity trends, and a calendar of holidays, expiries and rate decisions. Updated every 10 minutes while the market is open.

It helps judge how risky the market is, not which way it will go. Every reading on it was tested on 2015–2026 data: **[the research behind the page](https://markets.ashwinraghavan.com/research.html)**.

Downloads: [daily history since 2015 (CSV)](breadth.csv) · [today's sector table (CSV)](sectors.csv)

This repository holds only the published page, rebuilt each day; it has no history.
